# NextMove financial decisions

NextMove helps a customer understand financial consequences and carry out specifically authorized actions. The demonstration uses fictional financial institutions and merchants.

## Language

**Customer context**: A personal or business financial scope with its own funds, obligations, goals and authority.

**Goal**: A customer-confirmed financial outcome and its timing, protected reserve and preferences.

**Subscription contract**: A merchant service agreement for a particular subscriber account, distinct from a bank payment or card mandate.

**Renewal-off**: A confirmed change preventing the next applicable renewal while preserving already-paid access under the contract's terms.

**Proposal**: A reviewable, versioned set of suggested actions, consequences, conditions and expiry. It does not itself authorize execution.

**Approval mandate**: A customer's authority for an exact proposal/action manifest, its accounts, effects, limits and validity period.

**Batch**: A fixed selected set of proposed or approved actions, whose children retain separate outcomes and evidence.

**Receipt**: Account-bound evidence of an action's outcome, issuer, reference, effective date and consequences.

**Outcome unknown**: An operation may have committed but lacks conclusive evidence; it requires reconciliation before any further mutation.

**Avoided future charge**: A payment excluded from the same dated forecast because of a supported contractual change. It is not a bank credit.

**Credited recovery**: Money actually posted as a unique bank credit after a refund or other recovery.

**Labs**: A controlled simulation where advanced workflows act on fictional resources and retain the same authorization and isolation rules.
